Privacy Policy

Kutubkhana — Digital Library

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Kutubkhana is a digital library application that helps users organize physical-book records, capture book covers, and browse and read PDF documents. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

1. Information the app handles

Depending on the features you use, Kutubkhana may handle:

  • Sign-in information: information made available through Google Sign-In, such as your name, email address, and account identifier, to authenticate your account.
  • Book records: titles, authors, publishers, ISBNs, editions, categories, notes, and other book details you enter or generate.
  • Book images: photos captured for book covers and related book-management features.
  • PDF information: file names, document references/permissions, reading progress, and bookmarks for PDFs you select.
  • Diagnostics: limited technical or crash information if diagnostic tools are enabled in the installed version.

2. Local storage and offline features

Kutubkhana supports local book management and offline features. Book records, images, PDF reading progress, bookmarks, and other information may be stored on your device, depending on the feature and app configuration.

The on-device OCR feature processes supported images locally and does not require a cloud OCR service. PDF files selected through Android's folder/document picker are accessed using the permissions you grant. The PDF reader is intended to read those files locally; the app does not intentionally upload PDF contents as part of ordinary local reading.

Locally stored information may be lost if you uninstall the app, clear its data, lose access to the selected files, or lose the device, unless a separate backup mechanism applies.

3. Google Sign-In and authentication

Kutubkhana uses Google Sign-In. Google and the application's configured authentication provider (for example, Firebase Authentication, if configured) may process information needed to authenticate your account, subject to their own policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

4. ISBN and online book-metadata lookup

The app may identify books using ISBN information, barcode scanning, or related metadata features. If online metadata lookup is enabled and an internet connection is available, an ISBN query may be sent to third-party services such as Open Library or Google Books to retrieve matching book information. The queried ISBN may therefore be disclosed to the relevant service. This is separate from offline OCR.

  • Open Library Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

5. Camera and image processing

Camera access is used for book-cover capture and related library features. Images may be cropped, enhanced, and processed by on-device OCR to help extract book information. These features are intended for book management, not advertising.

6. Advertising and tracking

Kutubkhana does not display advertisements. The app is not intended to collect personal information for advertising. Any analytics, crash-reporting, or third-party SDK collection enabled in a particular release must be disclosed according to the actual behavior of that release.

7. Sharing and disclosure

Kutubkhana does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by Google, an authentication provider, or an external book-metadata service when you use the corresponding feature. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

8. Data security and retention

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the app, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Local information generally remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Third-party services may retain information under their own policies.

9. Children's privacy

Kutubkhana is a general library-management app and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe information has been collected inappropriately, please contact the developer.

10. Your choices

You choose what book information to enter and which folders or files to grant access to. You can revoke folder access through Android settings. Where online metadata lookup is optional, you can choose not to use it. You can manage your Google account through Google account settings.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published at this same URL with a revised effective date where appropriate.
